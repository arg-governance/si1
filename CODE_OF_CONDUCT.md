# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

The SI-1 project operates under the *Protokol Časne Saradnje* — Protocol of
Honorable Collaboration. We pledge to make participation in this project a
respectful and technically serious experience for everyone, regardless of
background, level of expertise, institutional affiliation, or model
preference.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- **Intellectual honesty.** Distinguishing claims from inferences, citing
  sources, and admitting uncertainty.
- **Constructive challenge.** Critiquing technical ideas on their merits,
  never the people who hold them.
- **Long-term thinking.** Optimizing for the durability of the project and
  the integrity of the field over short-term agreement.
- **Multi-perspective synthesis.** Considering cross-domain implications
  before shipping a change.
- **Recursive refinement.** Preserving inferential continuity across
  conversations and review cycles.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Personal attacks, harassment, or discrimination of any kind.
- Bad-faith framing of technical disagreement as personal conflict.
- Publishing private correspondence or unredacted logs without consent.
- Submitting contributions that intentionally weaken the constitutional
  guarantees of the runtime (see `docs/decisions.md` non-goals section).
- Submitting prompt-injection vectors disguised as test cases.

## The 8 Constitutional Tenets

All participation in SI-1 — including issues, pull requests, and review
comments — is held to the same 8 Constitutional Tenets the runtime enforces
for its models. They are listed in [README §XI](README.md).

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing standards
of acceptable behavior. Maintainers may remove comments, commits, code,
issues, and other contributions that do not align with this Code of Conduct.

Sanctions may include:

1. **Private warning** — for first-time, low-severity infractions.
2. **Public warning** — for repeated or higher-severity infractions.
3. **Temporary ban** — for sustained or serious infractions.
4. **Permanent ban** — for malice, deliberate harm, or violation of the
   constitutional guarantees.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces — GitHub issues,
pull requests, discussions, and any other communication channel officially
associated with the project. It also applies when a project participant
is representing the project in public spaces.
